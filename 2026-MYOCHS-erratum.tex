Our article~\cite{svozil-2025-MYOCHS} omits an attribution and contains the errors listed below.
None of them affects the main results.

\paragraph*{Attribution.}
As a hypergraph, each of the three $69$-ray, $50$-context lineages of Ref.~\cite{svozil-2025-MYOCHS} is the critical $69$-$50$ Kochen--Specker set reported earlier by Pavi\v{c}i\'{c} and Megill~\cite[Table~I]{pavicic-megill-2022} and given explicitly, with diagram, hypergraph string and complex coordinatization, by Pavi\v{c}i\'{c}~\cite[Fig.~10(e), Table~2, and Appendix~C]{Pavicic_2023}.
We have checked that all three lineages are isomorphic to it.
Removing its $36$ rays that occur in only one context leaves the $33$ rays and $14$ complete contexts of the set in Ref.~\cite{Cabello2025}; see also Refs.~\cite{Pavicic-2025-simplest,Pavicic-2026-entropy}.
Reference~\cite{svozil-2025-MYOCHS} should have cited Refs.~\cite{pavicic-megill-2022,Pavicic_2023}.

\paragraph*{Corrections.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Tables~1--3.}
The labels $e_1$, $e_2$, $e_3$ should read $e_2$, $e_3$, $e_1$, respectively.
Then they agree with Fig.~1, where $e_i=c_i\times b_{jk}$; for example, $e_{11}=(2,1,1)$.
The rays and contexts are unchanged.

\item \emph{Fig.~2(b).}
The node labeled $v_{1423}$ should carry the ray $(1,1,-1,1)$, which is Cabello's $v_{47}$ in Table~4, not $(1,-1,-1,1)$.
The latter is not orthogonal to the other rays of its two contexts.

\item \emph{Fig.~2, caption.}
``Solid curves show imposed contexts'' should read: ``Solid curves show the $13$ contexts of $\mathfrak{B}_{13}$, namely the $10$ imposed contexts and the three disjoint-support contexts $\mathcal{B}_{1234}$, $\mathcal{B}_{1324}$, and $\mathcal{B}_{1423}$.''

\item \emph{Table~4.}
Cabello's labels $v_{12}$, $v_{23}$ and $v_{34}$ coincide with gadget labels that denote different rays; for example, Cabello's $v_{12}$ is $(1,0,0,0)$, whereas the gadget's $v_{12}$ is $(0,0,1,-1)$.
Cabello's labels should carry a superscript, $v^{\mathrm{C}}_{ij}$.

\item \emph{Sect.~4.2.2, last paragraph.}
The statement that the six pair contexts and the three connector contexts ``form the 10-context scaffold that enforces Proposition~2'' is too strong.
Proposition~2 also uses the prescribed coordinates of $v_{1234}$, $v_{1324}$ and $v_{1423}$.
The incidences of these $10$ contexts alone do not force $u=(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)$ to be unbiased: for any $u$ with nonzero coordinates they are realized with $v_{1234}\propto(s_{34}x_1,s_{34}x_2,-s_{12}x_3,-s_{12}x_4)$, where $s_{kl}=|x_k|^2+|x_l|^2$, and analogously for $v_{1324}$ and $v_{1423}$.
The complete $17$-context hypergraph does force maximal unbiasedness by its incidences alone, through the contexts $\mathcal{E}_1$ and $\mathcal{E}_3$.
Likewise, in dimension five, Proposition~3 uses the prescribed coordinates of the connector rays $g_i$.

\item \emph{References.}
Ref.~[3] of Ref.~\cite{svozil-2025-MYOCHS} was published as Ref.~\cite{2024-cabello-trandafir0}, and Ref.~[24] has since appeared as Ref.~\cite{mcnulty2024mutually}.
\end{enumerate}

A corrected version of Ref.~\cite{svozil-2025-MYOCHS} is available as arXiv:2509.08636v3.
