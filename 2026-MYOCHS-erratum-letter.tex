\opening{\textbf{Re:} Correction to M.~Navara and K.~Svozil, ``Construction of Kochen--Specker Sets from Mutually Unbiased Bases,'' Int.\ J.\ Theor.\ Phys.\ \textbf{65}, 322 (2026), \url{https://doi.org/10.1007/s10773-026-06472-5}\\[2ex]
Dear Editors,}

On behalf of both authors, I ask you to publish a Correction (erratum) to the article above.
After publication we rechecked all calculations in exact arithmetic and found the errors listed below.
In addition, Mladen Pavi\v{c}i\'{c} pointed out that the article does not credit his earlier publication of the $69$-ray, $50$-context hypergraph; we correct this omission below.

None of them affects the main results.
The 165 rays and 130 contexts, the three 69-ray, 50-context lineages with 21 common rays and 10 common contexts, the absence of two-valued states for each lineage, and the state counts 24, 36, 36 and 16 all remain correct.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Errors and corrections}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Fig.~2(b).}
The node labeled $v_{1423}$ carries the wrong ray.
$v_{1423}=(1,-1,-1,1)$ is not in Cabello's set and is not orthogonal to the other rays of its two contexts; for instance, $\langle v_{13},v_{1423}\rangle=2$.
The correct ray is $(1,1,-1,1)=\{v_{13},v_{23},w_{12}\}^\perp$, which is Cabello's $v_{47}$ in Table~4.

\item \textbf{Fig.~1 versus Tables~1--3.}
The labels $e_1,e_2,e_3$ are cyclically permuted between Fig.~1 and the tables.
In Fig.~1, $e_i$ lies in the context $\{c_i,b_{jk},e_i\}$; in the tables, $e_1$ is paired with $c_2$, $e_2$ with $c_3$ and $e_3$ with $c_1$.
We relabel the tables so that $e_i=c_i\times b_{jk}$, in agreement with Fig.~1; for example, $e_{11}=(2,1,1)$.
The rays and contexts themselves are unchanged.

\item \textbf{Table~4.}
Cabello's labels $v_{12}$, $v_{23}$ and $v_{34}$ coincide with gadget labels that denote different vectors.
For example, Cabello's $v_{12}$ is $(1,0,0,0)$, whereas our $v_{12}$ is $(0,0,1,-1)$.
We now write Cabello's labels with a superscript C, as $v^{\mathrm{C}}_{ij}$, the ray shared by his contexts $i$ and $j$.

\item \textbf{Sect.~4.2.2, last paragraph (p.~12).}
The statement that the six pair contexts and three connector contexts ``form the 10-context scaffold that enforces Proposition~2'' is too strong.
Proposition~2 relies on the prescribed coordinates of $v_{1234}$, $v_{1324}$ and $v_{1423}$.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item The incidences of these 10 contexts alone do not force $u$ to be unbiased.
For any $u$ with nonzero coordinates they are realized with $v_{1234}\propto(s_{34}x_1,s_{34}x_2,-s_{12}x_3,-s_{12}x_4)$, where $s_{kl}=|x_k|^2+|x_l|^2$, and analogously for $v_{1324}$ and $v_{1423}$.
\item The complete 17-context hypergraph does force maximal unbiasedness by its incidences alone, through the contexts $\mathcal{E}_1$ and $\mathcal{E}_3$.
\item In $D=5$, Proposition~3 likewise uses the prescribed coordinates of the connector rays $g_i$.
\end{itemize}

\item \textbf{Fig.~2 caption.}
``Solid curves show imposed contexts'' should read: solid curves show the 13 contexts of $\mathfrak{B}_{13}$, namely the 10 imposed contexts and the three disjoint-support contexts $\mathcal{B}_{1234}$, $\mathcal{B}_{1324}$ and $\mathcal{B}_{1423}$.

\item \textbf{References.}
Ref.~[3] (Trandafir and Cabello) was published as Phys.\ Rev.\ A \textbf{111}, 022408 (2025).
Ref.~[24] (McNulty and Weigert) has since appeared in Quantum \textbf{10}, 2051 (2026).
\end{enumerate}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Missing attribution}

As a hypergraph, each of our three $69$-ray, $50$-context lineages is the $69$-$50$ Kochen--Specker set reported earlier by M.~Pavi\v{c}i\'{c} and N.~D.~Megill, Phys.\ Rev.\ A \textbf{106}, L060203 (2022), Table~I, and given explicitly, with diagram, hypergraph string and complex coordinatization, by M.~Pavi\v{c}i\'{c}, Quantum \textbf{7}, 953 (2023), Fig.~10(e), Table~2 and p.~55.
We have checked that all three lineages are isomorphic to it.
Our article does not cite these papers.
We ask that the Correction add them, together with M.~Pavi\v{c}i\'{c}, arXiv:2512.10483 (2025), as references [A], [B] and [C], and make the following additions.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Abstract.} After ``Each 69-ray, 50-context lineage is a KS configuration'' add: ``; as a hypergraph it is the 69-50 KS set reported earlier by Pavi\v{c}i\'{c} and Megill.''
The rest of that sentence should read: ``This framework clarifies the relation among the Yu--Oh, Harding--Salinas Schmeis, Pavi\v{c}i\'{c}--Megill, and Cabello constructions.''
\item \textbf{Sect.~1, item~1.} At the end add: ``As a hypergraph, each lineage is the 69-50 KS set reported earlier by Pavi\v{c}i\'{c} and Megill [A,~B].''
\item \textbf{Sect.~2, first paragraph.} After ``gives an isomorphic 69-ray, 50-context KS configuration.'' add: ``This hypergraph was found earlier. Pavi\v{c}i\'{c} and Megill listed a critical 69-50 KS hypergraph as the smallest one generated by the vector components $\{0,\pm\omega,2\omega,\pm\omega^2,2\omega^2\}$ [A, Table~I], and Pavi\v{c}i\'{c} gave its diagram, its hypergraph string, and a complex coordinatization [B, Fig.~10(e), Table~2, and p.~55]. We have checked that each of our lineages is isomorphic to that 69-50 hypergraph. Deleting its 36 nonintertwining rays leaves the 33 rays and 14 complete contexts of the set in Ref.~[14]; see also Ref.~[C].''
\item \textbf{Sect.~5, first paragraph.} The sentence beginning ``This places the triple'' should read: ``This places the triple of 25-ray full-context Yu--Oh completions, the Harding--Salinas Schmeis and Cabello descriptions, and the 69-50 hypergraph of Pavi\v{c}i\'{c} and Megill [A,~B] in a common MUB-based framework without assigning shared rays to an arbitrary first lineage.''
\end{itemize}

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Corrected version}

We will post the corrected article on arXiv as version~3 of arXiv:2509.08636.

The corrected manuscript, with all changes marked in blue, is attached.
