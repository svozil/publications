\section*{Professional Summary}
Distinguished Theoretical Physicist and Emeritus Professor (Ao.Univ.Prof. i.R.) at the Vienna University of Technology (TU Wien). Extensively published researcher with over 190 peer-reviewed works specializing in quantum mechanics, theoretical physics, quantum contextuality, randomness, and computer science logic. Recognized international academic leader, former President of the International Quantum Structure Association, and frequent collaborator with global academic and scientific institutions.
